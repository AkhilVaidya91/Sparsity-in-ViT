Observed result values

Immediate pruning. At 80% location-specific pruning, attention pruning falls to 0.83% accuracy, a 93.61 pp decrease from dense. MLP pruning at the same 80% level retains 42.48%, a 51.96 pp decrease.
Original initialization. The original-initialization retrained models exceed their corresponding random-reinitialized models At clean test accuracy, the largest original-minus-random gap is 9.52 pp for Attention pruning at 80%; the only negative gap is -0.74 pp for Mlp pruning at 80%.
Recovery after pruning. The largest recovery from pruning-only to retraining is 92.55 pp, occurring in attention pruning at 80% with original initialization (92.55 pp).
PGD at 8/255. The highest adversarial accuracy among the sparse/retrained conditions is 83.50% for Attention 20% Orig. init.; the lowest is 0.53% for Attention 80% Pruned. Dense accuracy under the same PGD budget is 20.20%.
