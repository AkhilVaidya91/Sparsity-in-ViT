PGD adversarial accuracy

10-step PGD with random start, step size ε/4. Color encodes accuracy on a shared 0 to 100% scale.

Lower accuracyHigher accuracy
At ε = 8/255, the dense baseline reaches 22.67% adversarial accuracy. Original-initialization models range from 23.90% to 31.73% (attention), 24.16% to 27.13% (MLP) and 25.90% to 30.21% (mixed). Random-reinitialization models range from 7.53% to 20.85% (attention) and 18.69% to 26.44% (MLP). Pruning-only models range from 0.34% to 22.27%, 18.99% to 22.38% and 21.03% to 22.00% for attention, MLP and mixed.
